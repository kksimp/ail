# Changelog

AIL is pre-1.0: `v0.MINOR.PATCH`, where MINOR may change the language or std and PATCH only fixes.
Entries are generated by `tools/release/changelog.sh` from merged pull-request titles; the
rules are in [ADR 1043](docs/decisions/1043-versions-tags-changelog.md).
Seeds keep their dated tags (`seed-YYYY.MM.DD[a-z]`) and are not releases.
